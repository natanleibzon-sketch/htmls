Social Proof is the #1 move separating contract calls from no-contract calls. Saying "we've helped many sellers just like you," "we have great reviews," or "our team has done this many times" appears in nearly 1 in 3 contract calls — but only 1 in 5 no-contract calls. It builds enough credibility to get a seller to say yes to the next step.
What counts as social proof
• "We've helped hundreds of sellers across St. Louis..."
• "We have great reviews, you can check us out online..."
• "Other sellers in your situation have found this really helpful..."
• "Our team has done thousands of transactions..."
• Mentioning specific neighborhoods/markets to establish local credibility
Why it works in this context
Motivated sellers are often skeptical — they've heard "we buy houses" ads everywhere. Social proof reduces perceived risk. It answers "are these people real and can I trust them?" before the seller even has to ask. It's the credibility unlock that lets everything else land.

Call Coaching

The In-Call Playbook — What the Data Says to Do

1️⃣
Open with Social Proof
Biggest C vs NC gap (+9.6pp). Before anything else, establish credibility. "We've helped thousands of sellers across [market], many in situations just like yours." Reduces skepticism, opens the door.
2️⃣
Lead with As-Is Framing
Double winner — +6.2pp C vs NC AND +8.3pp CL vs DE. Remove the repair anxiety immediately. "We buy as-is, you don't need to fix or clean a single thing." This single message unlocks both contract creation AND closing.
3️⃣
Validate Before You Evaluate
Empathy precedes curiosity in contracts (2x ratio vs no-contracts). Acknowledge the seller's situation before pivoting to business. "That sounds really difficult, I understand." Then ask questions.
4️⃣
Surface the Urgency — Don't Invent It
Sellers in contract calls are nearly 43% more likely to express real urgency (+12.7pp). Ask "what's your timeline?" and "what happens if this doesn't sell?" Listen for urgency cues. Then reflect them back.
🚫
Don't Lead With Price
Price anchoring is the only agent move with a NEGATIVE gap (NC uses it 25% vs C at 21%). Build value — as-is, flexible close, no fees, certainty — before the number lands. Frame price as "an offer you can feel good about."
5️⃣
Push for Appointment at Peak Openness
+5pp CL vs DE gap. When the seller signals moving forward — "sounds good," "I'm interested" — immediately push to a physical visit. "When can I come by?" Don't leave it abstract. Book it while energy is high.

Full Sequence Comparison

Anatomy of a Contract Call vs No-Contract Call

✅ Contract Call Pattern

1. Open Social proof + local credibility established
2. Qualify "Ask you a few basic questions" — brief property assessment
3. Empathy Acknowledge seller's situation — first shift (curiosity) unlocked at 33% mark
4. Frame As-is + flexible timeline + no fees — seller shift to urgency/price acceptance
5. Offer Price lands after value framing — "offer that you like" language
6. Push Appointment push at peak openness → "Moving Forward" signal → contract

❌ No-Contract Call Pattern

1. Open Jumps to qualification — no social proof, no credibility build
2. Qualify Extended question phase — "ask you a few questions" loops without advancing
3. Price Anchors a number early without framing value — seller rejects anchor
4. Stall Seller shows curiosity but agent doesn't convert it to momentum
5. No arc Resistance not resolved — seller stays at "need to think"
6. No push No appointment push — call ends without commitment

Bottom Line

5 Data-Backed Findings From 7,354 Calls

1. Momentum, not a single moment
Contract calls average 3.39 seller shifts vs 2.50 in no-contracts. There is no "magic line" — there's a rhythm of small yeses. The agent's job is to string them together.
2. Social Proof is the #1 contract creator
+9.6pp C vs NC — larger than any other move. Credibility-building ("we've done this hundreds of times") is the entry point. Without it, sellers stay skeptical no matter what comes next.
3. As-Is is the only double winner
Wins at both stages: +6.2pp getting contracts, +8.3pp closing them. "We buy as-is, no repairs needed" is the single most powerful consistent message across the entire sales process.
4. Anchoring price early kills deals
The only negative gap: no-contract calls use price anchoring more than contract calls. Building value (as-is, timeline, certainty, no fees) before introducing a number is the order that wins.
5. The "Moving Forward" signal is the contract predictor
+14.1pp — the largest single seller shift gap. When a seller says "let's do it," "sounds good," or "you can come" — in contracts, agents heard that moment 57% of the time vs 43% in no-contracts. These agents create the conditions for that moment.
